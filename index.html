About

AI systems research with performance close to the metal.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University. At NOWLAB, I work with Professor Dhabaleswar K. Panda on enhancing PyTorch for distributed deep learning with MPI.

My background spans high-performance deep learning, computer networking, operating systems, machine learning, and generative modeling. Before OSU, I completed my B.Tech at IIT Kharagpur with a micro-specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Applications.

Research

Current work and interests.

01

Distributed training systems

MPI-backed collectives for PyTorch, including reduce-scatter and all-gather-base support for FSDP.

02

HPC benchmarking

Performance studies across MVAPICH-Plus, Open MPI, MPICH, and Cray MPICH on AMD MI300A APU clusters.

03

Efficient AI inference

Memory-aware pruning, activation-aware quantization, and mixed precision for transformer inference on edge devices.
